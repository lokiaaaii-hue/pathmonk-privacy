Privacy policy for Monk’s Path

«Путь Монаха», «Путь Монаха: сила на турнике», Monk’s Path.
Application ID: ru.pathmonk.zaryadka. RuStore and Google Play versions.

Who is responsible for the app

The developer is Vladislav Gavrilov, an individual. The developer name used on Google Play is Falco von Valacius. For questions about data processing, contact L0mik8@yandex.ru.

Which version you use

The age and voluntary consent choices described below are provided in Google Play version 1.9.1 (28). In that version, ads and AppMetrica data sending are off by default and are enabled only after an adult gives consent.

The previously published RuStore version 1.9.0 (27) does not provide these choices: its advertising SDK and embedded analytics may process the technical data listed below without the new consent control. The withdrawal steps through “Ads and data choices” below do not apply to that older version. Ad display is disabled in the older Google Play test version 1.8.0 (19); this alone is not a statement that all embedded SDK telemetry is absent.

You can find the installed version in the app’s settings. The sections on the local journal, export, correspondence, retention and deletion apply to both store editions.

Overview

The app works without registration or an account. It stores your workout journal and settings in the app’s storage on your device. The developer does not operate a server to receive your journal. The app does not send your journal, notes or wellbeing entries to the advertising network.

In Google Play version 1.9.1 (28), ads are offered only to users aged 18 or older who give separate, voluntary consent. If you agree, Yandex and its embedded AppMetrica receive technical data for advertising and SDK diagnostics. Declining ads does not limit workouts. Data may also be sent to a service you choose when exporting or sharing content, to a store when rating the app, and to the email service and developer if you contact the developer by email. These situations are explained below.

Age group and advertising choices

In Google Play version 1.9.1 (28), the app offers four age choices: under 13, 13–17, 18 or older, or prefer not to say. It does not request your date of birth or an identity document for this purpose. Your group, advertising choice and notice version are stored only on your device, separately from the journal. The app does not send your chosen group to the advertising network. This record is excluded from journal exports and the app’s system backup. If there is no record, or the notice has materially changed, ads are off by default.

The app does not request ads for users under 18, users who do not provide an age group, or users who decline. Adults can separately allow ads and the related technical processing or continue without ads. Accepting the privacy policy alone does not enable ads. You can change your age group or withdraw consent in Settings → Ads and data choices.

Workout data on your device

To create your programme, track progress and schedule reminders, the app processes:

  • workout dates, exercises, sets and repetitions;
  • programme levels, experience, consolidation streaks and rank;
  • notes and wellbeing entries, including discomfort, overload and selected body areas;
  • equipment, language, reminder, sound and vibration settings.

This information remains on your device until you delete it. The developer has no remote access to it. Copies made by Android or your device manufacturer depend on your system backup and transfer settings and the terms of the selected system service.

When recovering from a damaged saved workout, the app may keep an original journal snapshot in its local storage to prevent data loss. These recovery copies are not automatically sent to the developer and remain until the app’s local storage is cleared.

Exporting, sharing and restoring

At your request, the app saves a journal backup to a file, shares it through an app you select, or prepares a progress image for sharing. A journal file may include your notes and wellbeing entries; take this into account when choosing a recipient. Progress images do not include notes or wellbeing entries.

When restoring a backup, the app reads the file you select. Clearing the app does not delete separately saved files, shared images or recipients’ copies. Storage and subsequent processing by an external service are governed by its terms and your settings.

Reminders

Reminders are generated on your device from your schedule and journal. The app does not send the journal to a server for this function. You may decline notification permission or turn it off in Android settings.

Advertising and AppMetrica

After an adult agrees, a Yandex banner may appear during rest between sets. The app uses Yandex Mobile Ads SDK with embedded AppMetrica. Advertising initialisation is deferred until the first eligible rest; no ads are requested before consent. In a build with advertising disabled, ad display and AppMetrica sending remain off regardless of a previously saved choice.

As part of the advertising SDK’s operation, the following categories are sent to Yandex:

  • Device or app installation identifiers, including SDK identifiers, for technical analytics and, depending on the identifier’s purpose and SDK settings, ad delivery and measurement.
  • Crash reports, including exception and failure information, to identify and fix errors.
  • Diagnostics, including information about unresponsiveness and SDK operation, to analyse stability and performance.
  • Other technical data, such as OS, app and SDK versions and device characteristics, to analyse SDK operation.

During a network request, Yandex infrastructure can also see the connection’s IP address. The app does not send the journal’s contents, notes, wellbeing entries or performed exercises to the advertising SDK. The developer has not enabled separate analytics of actions on app screens.

The Google Advertising ID permission (AD_ID) is excluded from the app; this does not mean that all other SDK identifiers are absent. Location permissions are excluded, and location tracking in the advertising SDK is off. Consent for personalised targeting is passed as “no”. That setting alone does not disable all technical requests and telemetry.

The embedded analytics includes a module for obtaining Google App Set ID. This identifier is intended for technical analytics and other non-advertising purposes permitted by Google, not for ad personalisation or measurement.

While consent remains in effect, technical processing may continue outside the time a single banner is displayed. On withdrawal, the app stops new ad requests, removes the banner and disables AppMetrica data sending. Network operations already started cannot be recalled. This does not delete information already sent to Yandex or clear the SDK’s local data. If you consent again, previously stored technical events may be sent. When installed through Google Play, the SDK may use Google Play Install Referrer to obtain information about the installation source; the app does not send the journal through this mechanism.

The recipient of advertising and analytics data is the provider of Yandex services. See the Yandex privacy policy, Yandex Mobile Ads data disclosures and AppMetrica data disclosures.

App ratings and stores

RuStore. The RuStore version uses the store’s rating module and its service telemetry. To operate and diagnose the module, RuStore may receive the SDK name, type and version, app package identifier and version, a technical SDK identifier, and the name and time of service events. The recipient can see the IP address of a network connection. You submit ratings and reviews in the store; the app does not pass the journal, notes or wellbeing entries to this module.

RuStore is operated by ООО «Много приложений». Its privacy policy describes retention according to processing purposes and applicable legal requirements, ending processing once its purposes have been achieved, and requests to correct, restrict or erase data. Clearing the journal in our app does not automatically delete RuStore’s data.

Google Play. The Google Play version opens the app’s listing in the external store app or browser. The RuStore SDK is not included in this version. Google processes your store account information and published review under its privacy policy.

You can turn off prompts to rate the app in its settings. This disables the prompt, not all telemetry from the advertising SDK.

Email enquiries

If you email the developer, they receive your sender address, message and any attachments you send, and use them to reply and investigate your enquiry. Do not send your journal or health information unless needed to resolve the issue. The email service processes correspondence under its own terms. Correspondence is retained while the enquiry and necessary follow-up are handled; it may be kept longer where required to meet applicable legal obligations.

Protection, retention and deletion

Access to local data is restricted by Android and your device’s security. Yandex documentation describes protection of SDK data in transit: Mobile Ads uses HTTPS/TLS, and AppMetrica also describes encryption in transit. You should not treat exported journal files as encrypted by this app.

Settings → Erase all data clears the current journal and progress. It does not clear all app storage: some settings, including rating preferences, your age group and advertising choice, and previously created recovery copies remain. To decline ads, use Ads and data choices. To clear all local data, including those copies, your advertising choice and local SDK data, use Android’s Clear storage / Clear data command for the app, or uninstall it. The command’s name varies by device.

Separately saved files, recipients’ copies and system backups are managed separately. Previously saved information may reappear when a backup is restored.

Clearing local data or uninstalling the app does not automatically delete information already sent to Yandex. Section 8 of Yandex’s privacy policy states that information is retained as needed for the purposes of collection or to meet legal and regulatory requirements. This is a retention criterion, not a fixed number of days after uninstalling the app.

The app has no built-in request to delete technical data from Yandex systems. To enquire about data processed by Yandex, use the contacts in its policy: personal-data@yandex-team.ru for Russia or gdpr@yandex-team.ru for other countries. Identify the app and specify that the enquiry concerns Mobile Ads / AppMetrica. Yandex handles requests under applicable rules; deletion through Yandex ID is available only where the relevant service provides the data and function.

For data you voluntarily emailed to the developer, you may use the contact at the start of this policy, including to request correction or deletion. Whether a request can be fulfilled depends on applicable processing and retention requirements.

Age audience

The app is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. In Google Play version 1.9.1 (28), users aged 13–17 can use it without ads. The age-group choice is explained above; it is not identity verification. If you believe that personal data of a child under 13 has been sent to the developer, please contact the address above.
